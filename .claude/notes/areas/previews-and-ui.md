# Previews and the control UI

Covers `src/app.rs`, `src/ui.rs`, `src/ui/` (menus, keys, settings, modern, preview), `src/dock.rs`, `src/preview_pace.rs`, `src/multiview.rs`, `src/history.rs`, `src/export.rs`.

Moved word for word from the Layout section of `CLAUDE.md` (commit 2589061). Read this before
working in these files, and keep it true when the code changes. A heading that says "Part of"
is a piece of a longer entry that covered several areas.

See also: the `src/ui/preview.rs` piece ends with `audio::LoadMeter` and `audio::cause` (the sound
mixer's load, `media-and-streams.md`'s area).

## Part of the `src/gpu.rs` entry: `src/ui/preview.rs`

  `src/ui/preview.rs`: every preview keeps the display's shape (`fit_picture`, `space`, `space_size`,
  `part_shape`, `outline`, `WholeViewport`: egui clamps a paint callback's viewport to the window, which
  squeezed the picture); `PreviewOutput::canvas` is the preview's shape, `picture` the canvas its layers are
  laid out on (the source picture for a `part_of`). `audio::LoadMeter` averages NewQ's mixing over
  `STEADY_BUFFERS` buffers, each capped at 1; `audio::cause`/`stream_errors_late()` tell the system calling
  late from slow mixing (`analysis::dropout_cause`).

- `src/app.rs`: windows and wiring. `src/ui.rs`: the control UI.
  Menus: `src/ui/menus.rs` draws the menu bar, the same in both interfaces (File, Edit, Cue with
  its `Add ▸` submenu, Window, Tools, Settings, Help); the menus' names are `settings::*_MENU` and
  every item a hint names is a `settings::MenuItem` (`TEST_MODE_ITEM`, `MEDIA_MANAGER_ITEM`,
  `STATISTICS_ITEM`, `TIMELINE_ITEM`, `TIMELINE_OPENS_ITEM`, `CART_ITEM`, `KEYS_ITEM`; `path()`
  makes the pointer), so a pointer follows a move. Each item goes through `menus::noted`
  (cfg(test): "Menu ▸ Item" and its rect in `menu_items`); `menus_tests.rs` clicks every item the
  0.26 menus had at its new place (`moved()`, both interfaces; `in_new_interface` says which classic
  Window items the new interface has not). The Window menu's middle is per interface:
  `classic_panes` (the pane ticks, Pop out/Dock back, New preview, Reset layout) or
  `new_interface_windows` (the views as `Action::ShowView`, the Timeline tick, which also goes to
  Editor, the Cart, which goes to Operator, `NEW_PREVIEW_WINDOW`, and the popped-out panes as ticks
  that close them); `every_window_item_in_the_new_interface_changes_what_is_drawn` clicks each
  through the whole window from four starting states.
  `src/ui/keys.rs`: `KEYS`, the keyboard table, which must equal README's Keyboard table (a test
  reads it, and checks every shortcut a menu prints is in it), and its window
  (`UiState::show_keys`, `Action::ToggleKeys`, Help ▸ Keyboard shortcuts). `Action::OpenLogFolder`
  (Help). The Displays page's *+ Add* scrolls to the new display (`UiState::reveal_display`).

## Leaving the show, saving, and dialogs (the main thread runs the show)

  **Nothing on the main thread waits for a person**: `App::pass` ticks the engine there (video
  frames, fades, pre-waits, network heartbeats). `src/ui/leave.rs`: the questions before the show is
  left, drawn in the window as an `egui::Modal` (both interfaces, and in each pop-out, whose
  `keyboard` also takes Ctrl+Q): `leave::ask(Leave::{Quit, New, Open, OpenFile(path)})` is called by
  `Action::Quit/New/Open`, `OpenShowFile(_, true)` (edited while the dialog was open; a handed-over
  show), a dropped `.newq`, and `UiState::quit_requested` (the main window's close button/Alt+F4, a
  Mac's app-menu Quit), taken at the start of `draw`. `UiState::question`: `Playing` (a quit while
  `engine.is_active()` or Show mode), `Unsaved(then)` (Save / Don't save / Cancel), `Saving(then)`
  (waits for `saving`, `save_next` and an open `dialog`, then goes on if the show equals
  `saved_json`, else asks `Unsaved` again: a failed or cancelled save never leaves), `Recover(path)`
  (the crashed session's copy, put up by `App::render_control` from `pending_recovery`). Buttons
  only set `UiState::answer` (never focusable, so Space/Enter stay GO; Esc, consumed by
  `leave::window` before `keyboard`, is Cancel), acted on by `leave::settle` at the very end of
  `draw` (so a follower, whose actions are dropped, and a pop-out can answer). A quit that needs
  no asking or was agreed to sets `UiState::quit_now`, which `render_control` acts on (Test Mode's
  shutdown, `net_test_flush`, `stop_all`, exit). `test_locked`: Quit goes straight to `quit_now`.
  Deliberately leaving clears the recovery copy (`leave::go`). `quit_would_ask` feeds the Mac's
  `macwin::set_quit_freely`. Tests: `src/ui/leave_tests.rs` (whole window by mouse, engine ticked
  between frames), including `no_system_dialog_is_shown_on_the_thread_that_runs_the_show`, which
  reads `src/` and fails on any `rfd::`/`pick_file`/`pick_folder`/`save_file(` outside
  `DIALOGS_ALLOWED_IN` (the dialog threads: `start_dialog`, `start_slide_dialog`,
  `start_deck_dialog`, timeline `keys::pick_file`; builders `media_dialog`/`show_dialog`; and the
  exceptions where nothing plays: `open_main_window`'s failure, `crash::install_in`'s fatal box,
  `main.rs::hold_node_folder`).
  Saving: `start_save` writes on a `save` thread; one asked for while another is being written is
  kept in `UiState::save_next` (the latest, path and a copy of the show) and started by `poll_save`
  when that one ends, with "Still saving: …" in the status bar. A failed save is
  `tell_save_failed` -> `UiState::save_failed`, a non-modal "Save failed" window (`leave`) and a
  status-bar problem. File dialogs: `start_dialog` (`DialogFor`, a `file-dialog` thread, polled by
  `poll_dialog`), including `DialogFor::RecordFolder` for a recording's Browse….

## Part of the `src/app.rs` / `src/ui.rs` entry: selection and the clipboard

  Selection state: `multi` only counts while `selected == multi_for` (`sync_selection` at the start of `draw` and `apply`, and `forget_multi` in `load_into`, `restore` and the test stash), so an old selection never returns when ids repeat in another show. The clipboard remembers the show it came from (`show_epoch`) and whether it was cut: `paste_cues` remaps a fade/stop/control cue's target to the copy, clears targets from another show, and gives a cut its old ids back. The right-click menu's Fire/Fade out/Stop name the clicked cue (`FireCue` etc.), not `selected`. Copying also writes a short text to the system clipboard, since egui-winit sends Paste only when it holds some text. In a group, `crossfade_into_next` applies only to pictures on the same display and layer, and the automatic fade-out waits for the crossfade only while it is coming or running (`running` set in `tick`).

Trouble = some window's seen misses in the last `WINDOW` (3 s, cleared at every level change) at least `MIN_SEEN` (2) and over `Strictness::share` (Relaxed 5 %, Strict 1 %; Off returns Full and resets, unless forced) of its new pictures (at least `LEAST_PICTURES`, 30), or a stall; it steps `Level` down (Full, Fps60, Fps30, Fps15, Fps5, Half (5, ½ size), Quarter (5, ¼), Paused) at most once a `WINDOW`, a stall or trouble right after a step up at most every `STEP_DOWN_EVERY` (1 s); up one after `PATIENCE` (2 s) without trouble, the wait doubling (to `PATIENCE_MOST` 20 s) when trouble follows a step up within `RELAPSE_WITHIN` (4 s: a stall at the next GO is no relapse), back after `FORGIVE_AFTER` (60 s) at Full. It learns when giving way doesn't help (a compositor hiccup, a lid, a basic display driver): trouble is counted (seen misses + stalls outside the grace); once a level has been stood at for `measure` (`MEASURE` 6 s, doubling to `MEASURE_MOST` 30 s) and is still in trouble (`troubled_at` within a `WINDOW`: stray misses under the line at a calm level are never judged; in practice Paused) with trouble there at least `HELPED_UNDER` (half) the way down's rate (`SLACK` 3), it jumps back to `ceiling` (Full) with a `Floor` (the rate since giving way): at or above the floor's level trouble steps down only on a `surge` (more than `SLACK` + `SURGE_FACTOR`x the rate over the time looked at: since the floor's `since`, at most `RECENT` 10 s, at least a `WINDOW`); a surge while `trying` (within `measure` of going up) is `TheirsAfterAll`: down, `measure` doubled, `ceiling` a level lower; a floor under Full tries the level above after `raise_wait` (`RAISE_FIRST` 20 s, doubling to `RAISE_MOST` 60 s). The rate is kept while the trouble goes on (`FLOOR_WINDOW`, never above what was `found`) and halves every `FLOOR_HALF_LIFE` (5 min) once it stops, forgotten under `FORGET_UNDER`. `Reading::forced` (`NEWQ_PREVIEW_PRESSURE`, a ‰ of new pictures late, against Relaxed's line when Off) is never learned and works under Off. `News` (`take_news`) and each step are logged by `App::pace_previews` with the worst window's seen misses and share; the notice says "Previews back: the displays' missed pictures aren't theirs" for `NOT_THEIRS_SAID`. ## Part of the `src/remote.rs` entry (a run of other files' notes): the cue sheet export

  `src/export.rs`: the cue sheet as CSV or a printable page (a timeline clip's Start, Track and Also
  on columns, its pre-wait left empty; marker rows, `Row::marker`, after the timeline's last clip).
